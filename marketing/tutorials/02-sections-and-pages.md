# Sections and pages

Sections are the headings of your docs menu. Pages go inside sections. A page can sit in more than one section, and sections can sit inside other sections.

## Create a section

Either:

- Go to **Waypost, Structure, Assign pages** and use the **New section** box on the right: type a name, choose where it goes under **Inside**, press **Create section**.
- Or go to **Waypost, Sections**, the standard WordPress screen, where you can also rename, describe, nest and delete sections.

A section with no published pages in it is hidden on the site, so you can prepare sections before their pages are ready.

## Add pages to sections

There are four ways, so use whichever suits the moment.

**Several pages at once:** go to **Waypost, Structure, Assign pages**.

1. Use the filters at the top: **Not in docs yet**, **Already in docs** or **All**, and the **Filter by title** box.
2. Tick the pages.
3. Choose a section and press **Add to section**.

**Move to section** replaces a page's current sections with the one you choose. **Remove from docs** takes the page out of the menu and docs search without deleting it.

**While editing a page:** in the block editor, open the **Doc sections** panel in the sidebar and tick the sections.

**From the Pages list:** use **Quick Edit** on one page, or tick several pages and choose **Bulk actions, Edit** to add them to a section in one go.

**Documents:** PDF and Word files join sections from the Media Library. See [Search inside PDF and Word files](07-documents.md).

## Make a section heading clickable

By default a section heading in the menu only opens and closes its list. To make it a link too:

1. Go to **Waypost, Structure, Section links**.
2. Next to the section, choose the overview page it should open, then press **Save**.

That page is then shown as the section heading instead of as an item inside the list. Only pages already in the section are offered.

## Drafts and private pages

Only published pages appear on the site. Drafts and private pages in a section stay hidden until you publish them, and so does a section that only holds drafts.

## Using posts or custom post types

Go to **Waypost, Settings, Content** and tick the content types that hold docs. Their items then appear on the Assign pages screen and get the Doc sections panel in the editor.
