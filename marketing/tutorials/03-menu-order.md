# Order the menu

Out of the box, sections and pages are listed alphabetically. To put them in the order readers should follow:

1. Go to **Waypost, Structure, Menu order**.
2. Drag rows by the handle on their left. Items move within their own group, so a page stays inside its section.
3. Press **Save order**.

Pages and sub-sections share one list inside a section, so you can put a sub-section between two pages.

The order also decides **previous and next** links: they follow the menu from top to bottom.

## Other buttons

- **Reset to A–Z** goes back to alphabetical order everywhere.
- **Rebuild menu cache** refreshes the menu if it ever looks out of date, for example after importing pages with another plugin. Normally Waypost does this by itself whenever pages or sections change.

## Nesting sections

To put a section inside another, edit it under **Waypost, Sections** and set its **Parent**. Then come back to Menu order to place it.

## Menu behaviour

In **Waypost, Settings, Navigation**, **Keep one top-level section open at a time** makes the menu fold the other sections away when a reader opens one. Untick it to let readers open as many as they like.
